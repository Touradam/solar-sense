## Product Showcase - Solar Sense

**Section Headline:** Introducing Solar Sense

**Description:**
Solar Sense is a low-cost, wireless device that ensures compliance with the latest NEC Rapid Shutdown requirements while enabling precise fault detection and performance monitoring at the panel level.

Unlike other systems that require multiple devices or complex wiring, Solar Sense combines everything into a single, smart, plug-and-play module suitable for both residential and commercial solar installations.

### Key Features

**⚡ Rapid Shutdown:**
Disconnects its host panel in a fraction of a second, ensuring NEC compliance and protecting first responders during emergencies.

**🔍 Health Monitoring:**
Built-in sensors continuously collect voltage, current, and temperature data to evaluate the health of the solar cell and detect early-stage degradation.

**📱 Real-Time Monitoring and Alerts:**
An intuitive mobile or web app displays real-time performance for each panel and sends automatic notifications when issues arise — enabling quick, informed maintenance decisions.

## How It Works

**Section Headline:** How Solar Sense Works

**Description:**
Solar Sense is a self-powered, module-level power electronic (MLPE) device composed of two main systems:

### System 1: Rapid Disconnect Module
- Responds to emergency commands or automatically detected hazards
- Disconnects panels within seconds to stop power flow and ensure safety

### System 2: Self-Analysis and Machine Learning Module
- Continuously monitors electrical and thermal data
- Uses a physics-informed machine learning algorithm to model a digital twin of the solar cell
- Detects abnormal conditions such as shading, soiling, or cell degradation before damage occurs

**Conclusion:**
By combining these functions, Solar Sense transforms traditional solar panels into intelligent, self-aware energy units capable of ensuring both compliance and reliability.
